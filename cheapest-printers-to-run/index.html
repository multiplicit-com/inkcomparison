Cheapest printers to run

356 printer prices, checked 27 Nov 2026, 12:34

What a printer costs over its life depends far more on its ink than on its price. These are printers on sale now, ranked by what a page costs with original cartridges at today's cheapest prices. Compatibles, where we list them, usually bring it down further. Ink tank printers often top the list, as bottles hold far more ink: see ink tank printers.

How we work it out: the cheapest single cartridge of each colour divided by the page yield shops quote for it (the ISO figure for 5% coverage). A colour page counts every colour the printer uses. Your own costs depend on what you print.
