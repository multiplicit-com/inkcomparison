About HP

HP began in 1939, when Bill Hewlett and David Packard set up in a garage in Palo Alto, California. Today's HP Inc. was formed in 2015, when the original company split in two and HP Inc. kept the PCs and printers. It's one of the biggest names in home printing in the UK, with DeskJet, ENVY and OfficeJet inkjets, Smart Tank ink tank printers and LaserJet lasers. It also took over Samsung's printer business in 2017.

The one thing to know before buying: many HP printers use dynamic security, which can reject non-HP cartridge chips, and printers set up with HP+ only accept original HP ink.

What HP printers do well

  • A huge range, from cheap home inkjets to office lasers
  • OfficeJet Pro models are strong all-rounders for home offices
  • HP Instant Ink subscriptions can suit steady colour printers
