About Xerox

Xerox is the company that made photocopying a household word. Founded in Rochester, New York, in 1906, it launched the Xerox 914, the first plain-paper copier, in 1959, and a Xerox researcher invented the laser printer in 1969. Today Xerox concentrates on office printers and copiers, and it bought Lexmark in 2025.

What Xerox printers do well

  • Office multifunction printers and copiers built for heavy use
  • Colour lasers for business documents and graphics
