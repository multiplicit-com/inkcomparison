About OKI

OKI is one of Japan's oldest technology companies, founded in Tokyo in 1881 and originally a maker of telephones. Its printers use LED rather than laser technology, and it's long been known for tough dot matrix printers used in warehouses and offices. In recent years OKI has narrowed its focus to colour LED printers for business and specialist uses, and it stopped selling printers in the Americas in 2021.

What OKI printers do well

  • Colour LED printers for offices and specialist businesses
  • Dot matrix printers for multi-part forms
