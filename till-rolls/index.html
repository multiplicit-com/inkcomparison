Which width do you need?

The width is the one number that must match. The diameter only needs to fit the roll holder: a bigger roll lasts longer between changes.

WidthCommon sizesUsually for
57mm57×40, 57×30, 57×57Card machines (PDQ terminals), portable and mobile receipt printers, small tills
80mm80×80, 80×70EPOS receipt printers in shops, bars and restaurants
76mm76×76, 76×70Kitchen printers and older cash registers that print with an ink ribbon, often on 2-ply paper
44mm and 38mm44×70, 38×70Adding machines and printing calculators
112mm112×80 and similarWide receipt printers, some scales and medical equipment

Your machine's manual, or the label on an old roll's box, gives the size. If in doubt, measure the width of a roll that fits.

Thermal or plain paper?

  • Thermal rolls are used by almost all card machines and modern receipt printers. Heat darkens the paper, so there's no ink or ribbon to buy. A quick test: scratch the paper with a fingernail, and thermal paper leaves a dark mark.
  • Plain (1-ply) rolls are for printers that use an ink ribbon.
  • 2-ply rolls print a top copy and a carbonless copy underneath, often white and yellow. Kitchens use them so one copy goes to the chef and one stays with the order.

Thermal paper with BPA in it has been restricted in the UK and EU since 2020, so most rolls are now BPA-free. Some go further and say phenol-free.

Till rolls come in boxes of 5 to 50 or more, so we show the price per roll where the shop gives the pack size. Roll length varies between brands of the same size, so a roll that's slightly dearer can still last longer.
