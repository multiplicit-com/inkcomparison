Kodak ESP 5210 drivers and setup

Drivers and the user guide for the ESP 5210 are on Kodak's own support site. Only download drivers from the maker's site. If it's on Wi-Fi, phones and Macs can usually print without a driver (see printing from your phone).

Original or compatible cartridges?

Compatible toner for the Kodak ESP 5210 usually costs far less than Kodak's own and prints much the same for everyday documents. Check the page yield and the shop's guarantee. More in are compatible cartridges safe?
